How it works

  • Each trial starts on the Home screen after the task's apps are reset to a verified root view.
  • Every step, the model gets the goal, the screen's accessibility elements and a screenshot, and picks one action from a generic list: tap any element, type a task string, scroll, back, Home, open any of 26 apps, wait, FINISH or INFEASIBLE.
  • No step budget. Trials end on FINISH/INFEASIBLE, a 240 s limit, or 6 identical actions on an unchanged screen.
  • Pass is decided by a programmatic check of the phone's final state, never by the model. Every failure is audited by hand.
